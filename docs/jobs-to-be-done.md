# Jobs To Be Done

Jobs To Be Done describe the outcome a user seeks, not merely a feature request. Every job is linked to an app role.

| Role | When… | I want to… | So I can… | Product outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employee | I receive a job offer or pay-rise proposal | enter my salary, pension, deductions, and benefits | understand what I will receive after deductions | Clear annual and monthly take-home estimate with a transparent breakdown. |
| Employee | I am deciding between offers | compare packages side by side | choose the best outcome for my financial priorities | Net pay, pension, benefits, and employer-cost differences in one view. |
| Employee | My pay crosses a tax threshold | understand the deduction impact | negotiate with realistic expectations | Tax bands, Personal Allowance taper, and effective deduction-rate explanation. |
| Employee | I am changing pension contributions | compare salary sacrifice with relief at source | decide with an understanding of pay and pension impact | Distinct pension-method and contribution estimates. |
| Candidate | I am preparing for negotiation | model possible packages privately | set a realistic target | Named, editable, browser-local scenarios that can be exported. |
| Recruiter / HR editor | I need to explain an offer | create a standardised illustration | communicate the whole package consistently | Future shared templates and comparison summaries. |
| Hiring manager | I need to select an offer package | review an authorised comparison | make a budget-aware decision | Future view/edit permissions for explicitly shared scenarios. |
| Organisation admin | My organisation needs controlled offer modelling | manage templates and access | protect compensation information and reduce inconsistency | Future workspace roles, approved templates, and audit records. |
| Tax-rule administrator | HMRC changes rules | publish approved calculations | maintain traceable rule accuracy | Future versioned rules with approval, sources, and rollback. |
| Platform administrator | I operate a shared service | monitor and investigate problems | keep the service dependable while protecting privacy | Audited operational access under least privilege. |
